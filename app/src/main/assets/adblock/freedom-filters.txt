! Description: Freedom Browser's own fixes for sites upstream lists don't

!   Origin's filters. Maintained by hand in this repository — not

! #391 spiegel.de: an inline, obfuscated ad-reinsertion script probes
! brwsrfrm.com, criteo, ad-delivery.net, btloader.com and ag.dns-bait.com
! and, when they fail, injects a first-party image ad ("Anzeige").
! uBlock's `spiegel.de##+js(rmnt, script, homad)` no longer matches it;
! its probe URLs are hard-coded, so match those.
spiegel.de##+js(rmnt, script, /brwsrfrm|dns-bait/)

! #405 spiegel.de: every ad slot carries data-advertisement="pos_N …" and
! keeps a min-height (up to 632px on the front page, 352px in articles)
! empty once its ad is blocked. EasyList's
! spiegel.de##div[data-advertisement$=":mobile"] misses the front page's
! <section> slots and the article slots whose value ends ":desktop";
! EasyList Germany has nothing for them.
spiegel.de##[data-advertisement]
! Its first-party sponsored teasers ("Anzeige"): no network rule reaches them.
spiegel.de##[data-area^="article_teaser>sponsored"]
